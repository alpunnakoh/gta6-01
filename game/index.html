Follow the yellow marker. Stealing cars, shooting and hitting pedestrians raise your wanted level — hide from the police until the stars drop. At 3 stars the PD chopper joins the hunt; at 4 stars they throw a roadblock across the road ahead. Green packages around the city pay $150 each, and the sun sets, rises and sets again while you play. Hit a yellow ramp fast for a $50 stunt jump, and drive into the Pay 'n Spray garage to fix your car and lose the heat for $200. Finish a job with no stars at all for a clean-getaway bonus. Your progress saves automatically. After dark the beach road lights up with clubs and a night market — and taxi fares pay 1.5x. Rain and storms make the roads slick, so a wet getaway needs a gentler foot. Ten golden Sunset Relics hide around the city (the fifth unlocks the rifle); three street races wait at the striped START gates; and after the first six jobs, the crew is waiting at Port North for the Sunset Heist.
